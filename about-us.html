Our Mission

Honest Repair, Fair Prices & No Nonsense.

We started in 2012 with a simple belief: Apple device owners deserved better than being told to buy new when a £99 repair would do. The Apple Store's flat-rate out-of-warranty pricing — often £600+ for a logic board repair — was leaving too many people stranded.

Marcus Webb, our founder, left his role at Apple's Genius Bar and opened a 200 sq ft workshop on Clerkenwell Road with two colleagues and one mission: fix every Apple device that came through the door, at a fair price, with parts that matched Apple's own quality standards.

Twelve years later we occupy 4,000 sq ft of dedicated workshop space, employ 50+ Apple-certified engineers, and have completed over 18,000 repairs. The mission has not changed at all.

Our Values

Radical Honesty

We tell customers exactly what is wrong and what it costs — before we start. No upselling, no phantom faults, no inflated quotes. If we cannot fix it, we say so upfront.

Technical Excellence

Apple ACMT certification is our baseline, not our ceiling. We invest in equipment, training, and continuous learning to stay ahead of every new Apple architecture.

Repair First

We believe in extending the life of devices rather than replacing them. Every successful repair is a win for the customer, for their wallet, and for the planet.

Community First

We offer discounted repairs for students, NHS staff, and charities. Technology should be accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford Apple's prices.
